# Design System: VestWise

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Marked-Up Agreement"**

The page is one document: a sample VSOP grant agreement, set clause by clause in a book serif, that VestWise marks up in its own voice. The reader fills in its blanks; VestWise answers in the margin, in euros; a highlighter picks out the words that matter, a redline strikes a share count into its euro value, and the reader's signature line carries the action. Two typefaces play the two parties. Source Serif 4 is the agreement. Archivo, heavy and widened, is VestWise. Whatever the reader can change is a blank inside a sentence, and whatever VestWise computes from it is a figure in VestWise's voice; the key figures roll to their new values as the terms change.

The materials are paper, ink and one highlighter. The sheet is a near-neutral copy-paper white by day and a graphite sheet at night; the ink is a cool near-black with two receding greys; the highlighter is a fluorescent yellow that only ever appears as a flat, full-strength field. There are seven tokens per theme and nothing between them. Structure comes from 1px ink rules and whitespace measured on one vertical unit, the clause line. There are no cards, no shadows, no gradients and no rounded corners: a rule opens each part, a vertical rule divides the terms from VestWise's answer, and the highlighter, not elevation, says where to look.

Motion follows reading. A surface may open with one authored sequence (the letterhead rule draws, the headline unmasks, the highlighter sweeps, the key figure rolls up from zero); after that, motion is tied to reading: the clause in view deepens to full ink, phrases are highlighted as they are read, the annex draws itself once, and the closing redline strikes through. Every animated element rests in its finished state, so reduced motion, or a browser without scroll timelines, gets the finished page.

**Key Characteristics:**

- One document: clauses, blanks, margin glosses, an annex, a redline and signatures.
- Two voices: Source Serif 4 for the agreement, Archivo at 800 weight and 112% width for VestWise.
- Paper, ink and one highlighter: seven OKLCH tokens per theme, swapped wholesale by `prefers-color-scheme`.
- 1px ink rules on a single vertical unit, the clause line (2rem, 2.5rem from 1024px); every corner square.
- Computed figures only: tabular, formatted in the reader's locale, the key ones rolling digit by digit.
- Flat: no cards, shadows, blur or gradients.
- Motion rests in its final state and runs only under `prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference`.

## Colors

A monochrome legal sheet, ink on paper, with a single fluorescent highlighter; every color is an OKLCH custom property on `:root`, and dark mode replaces the whole set under `prefers-color-scheme: dark` (the page follows the system; it has no theme toggle).

### Primary

- **Highlighter Yellow** (`mark`, oklch(0.925 0.19 104), about #FCEB21; night `mark-dark`, oklch(0.9 0.18 104), about #F2E32F): the system's only chroma. A flat field behind the one word a headline turns on, behind the reader's key figure, behind a phrase as it is read, behind "this year" in the yearly ledger and "Today" in the exhibit. It fills the primary button, a focused blank and a checked tickbox, colors text selection and the bar in the wordmark, and can carry a whole part as a highlighter field.

### Neutral

- **Copy Paper** (`paper`, oklch(0.975 0.004 106), about #F7F7F4; night `paper-dark`, **Night Sheet**, oklch(0.18 0.006 270), about #101114): the sheet. Page and root background, scrollbar track, the ground of the running head, and the label of a hovered primary button.
- **Toner Black** (`ink`, oklch(0.205 0.008 270), about #16171B; night `ink-dark`, **Night Ink**, oklch(0.955 0.004 106), about #F0F0ED): running text and every rule; the vested area of the exhibit, blank underlines, the tickbox border, the redline bar, focus rings and the caret.
- **Soft Toner** (`ink-soft`, oklch(0.4 0.008 270), about #46484C; night oklch(0.8 0.006 270), about #BCBEC2): secondary copy. Ledes under headlines, gloss details, captions, the note under a figure, exhibit year labels and the running head's sentence.
- **Receded Grey** (`ink-muted`, oklch(0.52 0.008 270), about #67696E; night oklch(0.67 0.006 270), about #949599): what has stepped back. The "(sample terms)" aside, the struck share count, the other party's signature placeholder, the margin footnote, the exhibit's total label, an invalid blank's underline, the scrollbar thumb, and the resting tone of a clause outside the reading focus. It is the lightest text step and holds 5.1:1 on paper (6.3:1 at night).
- **Gridline Grey** (`hairline`, oklch(0.87 0.004 270), about #D3D4D7; night oklch(0.34 0.006 270), about #37383B): the exhibit's half and full gridlines only. Never text, never a divider between content.
- **Mark Ink** (`mark-ink`, oklch(0.205 0.008 270), about #16171B; night oklch(0.18 0.006 270), about #101114): the ink that sits on the highlighter. It equals `ink` by day and the night sheet at night, so anything on yellow is dark in both themes.

By day the paper carries the warm hue (106) and the inks the cool one (270); at night they trade, and the ink takes the paper's warmth. The favicon and the `theme-color` metas are the sRGB renderings of these tokens (#FCEB21 and #16171B; #F7F7F4 by day, #101114 at night).

### Named Rules

**The One Highlighter Rule.** `mark` is the only chroma in the system, and at rest it is always a flat, full-strength field with `mark-ink` on it: never tinted, translucent, outlined, rounded or graded, never a glow or an underline stroke. When a mark moves, a clip-path wipe moves its edge; its strength never animates.

**The Mark-Ink Rule.** Whatever sits on yellow is `mark-ink`: words, figures, rules, ticks, icons, focus rings and fine print. A highlighter field has no greys. Inverting it (a `mark-ink` block with `mark` figures) is the only way to emphasise something already on the field.

**The Never Alone Rule.** On the day sheet the yellow is a change of hue, not of value (about 1.15:1 against paper), so it never carries meaning by itself: every mark has `mark-ink` words, a label or an icon on or beside it.

**The Night Sheet Rule.** At night the yellow is about 14:1 against the sheet, so a highlighter field is held to the sheet's measure (the 1320px frame, inside the gutters) instead of bleeding to the viewport edge.

**The Seven Tokens Rule.** There is no tonal scale. New work uses the seven tokens of the theme; the only derived color in the system is the exhibit's 7% `ink` wash for shares still to vest.

## Typography

**Display Font:** Archivo Variable (with ui-sans-serif, system-ui, sans-serif), self-hosted from `@fontsource-variable/archivo/wdth.css`: weight 100 to 900, width 62% to 125%.
**Body Font:** Source Serif 4 Variable (with ui-serif, Georgia, serif), self-hosted from `@fontsource-variable/source-serif-4/opsz.css`: weight 200 to 900, optical size automatic (`font-optical-sizing: auto`). It sets the agreement; interface copy is Archivo at 100% width.
**Label/Mono Font:** none. Numbers use Archivo's tabular lining figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums lining-nums`).

**Character:** A book serif with optical sizing reads as the contract; a heavy grotesque widened to 112% answers it like a marker in the margin. Width does as much work as weight: the wider the setting, the more it is VestWise speaking.

### Hierarchy

The app adds fixed steps for a product screen (frontmatter `app-*`): the page title at 2rem, 2.25rem from 1024px; a section headline at 1.5rem, 1.75rem from 1024px; figures at 1.375rem and 2rem beside the landing's 1.75rem; the grant clause at 1.1875rem, 1.375rem from 1024px, and 1.125rem where a grant describes itself on the dashboard; interface copy at 1.0625rem; a large input at 1.25rem.

Frontmatter sizes are the composed values at 1024px and up. Where the code switches size at 1024px, the smaller value is given first. Headings balance their lines (`text-wrap: balance`); paragraphs wrap with `text-wrap: pretty`.

- **Display** (Archivo 800, width 112%, clamp(3.25rem, 8.2vw, 7.5rem), line-height 0.96, -0.03em, max 16ch): the surface's one headline. From 1024px its lines are composed as blocks; below, it wraps freely.
- **Headline** (Archivo 800, width 112%, clamp(2.25rem, 4.6vw, 4rem), line-height 0.96, -0.03em, 14 to 16ch): section headlines, always a whole sentence ending in its full stop or question mark. Set beside a clause, it steps down to clamp(2.25rem, 4.4vw, 3.75rem).
- **Headline Lead** (same voice, clamp(2.5rem, 5.6vw, 5rem)): the headline on a highlighter field. The closing statement uses clamp(2.5rem, 5vw, 4.5rem).
- **Figure Lead** (Archivo 800, width 112%, tabular lining, line-height 1, -0.03em, clamp(3rem, 5.2vw, 4.75rem)): the reader's key euro figure in the margin. The payout on the field runs to clamp(3rem, 6vw, 5.25rem). Each is sized for a typical length (7 to 9 characters) and shrinks in proportion beyond it.
- **Figure** (same voice, 1.75rem; 2rem for the margin's second figure from 1024px; 1.375rem for ruler readouts below 640px): secondary figures, yearly totals, ruler readouts. The redline's euro figure is clamp(1.625rem, 3vw, 2.75rem).
- **Clause Lead** (Source Serif 4 400, 1.1875rem then 1.625rem, line-height of one clause line): the clause that holds the blanks.
- **Clause** (Source Serif 4 400, 1.125rem then 1.3125rem, line-height 0.85 of the clause line, 52 to 62ch): every other clause; its § head runs in at 600.
- **Sub-clause** (Source Serif 4 400, 1.0625rem then 1.25rem, line-height 1.625): numbered sub-clauses, the number at 600.
- **Lede** (Archivo 400, 1.0625rem then 1.1875rem, line-height 1.625, `ink-soft`, 40 to 48ch): the plain sentence under a headline.
- **Gloss** (Archivo 700 at width 108%, 1rem, line-height 1.375, then a 400 sentence in `ink-soft`): VestWise's margin note, such as "No account. Open it and start."
- **Label** (Archivo 600, 0.9375rem): figure labels, the annex caption, year labels, signature labels; 1rem for ruler labels and the payout label. Letterhead links and the margin figure's label are 500.
- **Button** (Archivo 700, width 108%, 0.9375rem; 1.0625rem large).
- **Blank** (Archivo 650, width 106%, 0.9em of its clause, tabular lining, line-height 1.25).
- **Caption** (Archivo 400, 0.875rem): signature captions, yearly details, footer links.
- **Fine** (Archivo 400, 0.8125rem, line-height 1.625): disclaimers and footnotes.
- **Micro** (Archivo 0.75rem): exhibit labels and ruler ticks, tabular on the axes and ticks; the "Today" tag and the exhibit's marker titles at 700.
- **Wordmark** (Archivo 800, width 112%, -0.02em): "VestWise" after a 0.42em by 0.95em bar of `mark`, 0.45em apart.

### Named Rules

**The Two Voices Rule.** The agreement speaks only in Source Serif 4: clauses, § heads, sub-clauses, the struck share count, the other party's signature placeholder. It capitalises its defined terms (Virtual Shares, Strike Price, Cliff) and writes amounts as "EUR 4.00". VestWise speaks only in Archivo: headlines, figures, glosses, labels and controls, in plain lowercase terms and with the € sign. A blank is VestWise's voice set into the agreement's sentence.

**The Width Ladder Rule.** Archivo carries hierarchy on its width axis as well as its weight: 100% for running text, 106% for blanks, 108% for buttons and gloss heads, 112% for display, figures and the wordmark. Nothing is set narrower than 100% or wider than 112%, and nothing tracks tighter than -0.03em.

**The Tabular Rule.** Every number a reader compares or watches change is set in tabular lining figures: figures, blanks, ledger values, the exhibit's axis labels, ruler ticks and the running head's figure.

**The One Word Rule.** A headline highlights at most one word: the word the sentence turns on, at its end, with the full stop inside the mark ("VSOP.", "euros."). Most headlines carry none. In running text a mark may cover a short phrase ("where you are today").

**The Run-in Rule.** § numbers are the agreement's own structure. They run in at the start of their clause in serif 600 ("§ 2 Vesting."), sub-clauses as "4.1"; a § number is never a label on its own line above a headline.

## Layout

The page is one sheet in a 1320px frame. The letterhead, the running head, every part and the footer are centred at max 1320px with gutters of 1.25rem, 2rem from 640px and 3rem from 1024px. A highlighter field may bleed past the frame on the day sheet; at night it sits inside it.

**The clause line.** `--line` is 2rem, 2.5rem from 1024px. It is the clause leading (one line for the lead clause, 0.85 for the others) and it measures the distances between blocks: 1 line between blocks and grid rows; 1.25 lines from the letterhead to the display, between a headline group and what follows, and between stacked groups on small screens; 1.5 lines before an exhibit; 2 lines inside the closing; 3 lines of padding between parts (2.5 on a highlighter field below 1024px). Inside a block, distances use the 0.25rem step: 0.5rem, 0.75rem, 1rem and 1.5rem between a headline and its lede, a label and its figure, a figure and its note, or the rows of a list.

**Terms left, answer right.** Small screens get one column. From 1024px the frame is a 12-column grid with a 3rem gap, and a split puts the terms or the inputs left and VestWise's answer right, divided by a 1px vertical rule with 2.5rem between the rule and the answer: 8 + 4 for a clause and its margin figure or gloss, 5 + 7 for a clause beside VestWise's headline, 6 + 6 for the rulers and the ledger on a highlighter field. Below 1024px the answer follows its terms: a margin figure under a 1px horizontal rule with 1.5rem above its content, a gloss directly under its sub-clause.

**Breakpoints.** 640px (gutters, three yearly columns, larger ruler readouts, the running head's sentence), 768px (letterhead links, two signature columns, the footer row) and 1024px (the 12-column grid, the 2.5rem line, composed headline lines).

**Measures.** Headlines 14 to 16ch; ledes 40 to 48ch; clauses 52 to 62ch; margin notes 30 to 34ch; fine print 56 to 70ch.

**Parts.** A part opens with a full-width 1px ink rule, or with the change of ground to a highlighter field. Linked parts keep 3.5rem to 4rem of scroll margin so the running head never covers their first line.

### Named Rules

**The Clause Line Rule.** Distances between blocks and parts are multiples of `--line` (0.85, 1, 1.25, 1.5, 2, 2.5 or 3); the 0.25rem step is only for distances inside a block.

**The Terms Left Rule.** In any split the terms or the inputs sit left and VestWise's answer sits right of a 1px rule.

## Elevation & Depth

The system is flat. There are no shadows, no blur, no gradients and no stacked surfaces: separation is a 1px rule or whitespace, emphasis is the highlighter, and the vertical rule does the work a column of cards would do elsewhere. The one element that floats, the running head, floats on `paper` above a 1px ink rule. The only `box-shadow` in the code is not elevation at all: it draws a blank's underline inside its box (`inset 0 -2px 0`), so the underline can thicken on hover and focus without moving the line of text.

### Named Rules

**The Rule, Not Shadow Rule.** Whatever must separate or float gets a 1px ink rule (2px above a total) and the sheet behind it; never a shadow, a blur or a second surface color.

## Shapes

Every corner is square (0px): buttons, blanks, the tickbox, the ruler thumb, the "Today" tag, the inverse payout block and every highlighter field. Lines are the form language.

- **Rules:** 1px `ink`, horizontal and vertical; on a highlighter field 1px `mark-ink`, and 2px above a total.
- **Underlines:** a blank's 2px underline (4px on hover, 3px in `mark-ink` when focused); text links at 1px with a 0.22em offset, 2px on hover.
- **Strokes:** the tickbox's 1.5px border; the ruler's 2px track and its 0.625rem by 2.25rem slab of a thumb; the redline's bar, max(2px, 0.07em) thick, 56% of the way down its line, reaching 0.04em past the words at each side.
- **The highlighter rectangle:** the full height of its line box, tight to the words with 0.07em at each side, overhanging only to the left so the punctuation after it keeps its space.
- **The wordmark bar and favicon:** a 0.42em by 0.95em upright of `mark` before the name; the favicon is a `mark` square carrying a 4px `ink` V with square caps and a mitred point.
- **Inside the exhibit only:** dashed verticals (3 4) for the cliff and the fully vested date, a dashed (5 4) line for a second grant's total, and a 45° hatch of 2px `ink` lines every 6px for that grant's vested shares.

## Components

### Buttons

- **Character:** a flat block of yellow with a heavy label and an arrow; the place to sign.
- **Shape:** square (0px); 2.75rem high with 1.25rem side padding; large, 3.5rem high with 1.75rem padding and 1.0625rem text. The running head uses a 2.5rem-high instance.
- **Primary (mark):** `mark` field, `mark-ink` label in Archivo 700 at 108% width, then a 0.6em gap and an arrow at 1.1em (stroke 2.25).
- **Hover / Focus / Active:** with a fine pointer, hover inverts to `ink` with a `paper` label (160ms) and moves the arrow 3px right; a press scales to 0.97 (120ms); keyboard focus is the global 2px `ink` ring at 3px offset.
- **Ink:** a `mark-ink` field with a `mark` label that inverts to `paper` with an `ink` label on hover, for an action placed on a highlighter field (the current page places none there).
- **Skip link:** the primary button, hidden until focused, fixed 0.75rem from the top left.

### Text links

- **Style:** underlined at 1px with a 0.22em offset in the current text color; a fine-pointer hover thickens the underline to 2px (120ms). Letterhead links keep the underline transparent until hover. Links that leave the site end in an up-right arrow (1rem, stroke 2). A text-link button ("Reset to the sample terms") may sit beside the primary button on a signature line.

### Blanks (Inputs / Fields)

- **Character:** the agreement's fill-in fields. A blank sits in the sentence; it is never a box.
- **Style:** transparent, Archivo 650 at 106% width, 0.9em of the clause, tabular lining, with a 2px `ink` underline drawn inside the box, 0.2em of side padding and 0.06em of margin; the width follows the content in ch (2 or 3 characters at least); numbers are centred. A select blank is left-aligned with a 0.6em chevron (stroke 2.25), and its options are Archivo on `paper`.
- **Hover:** the underline thickens to 4px (fine pointer only).
- **Focus:** the blank becomes a highlighter field, `mark` behind `mark-ink` text over a 3px `mark-ink` underline, with its contents selected; keyboard focus adds a 2px `ink` ring at 2px offset.
- **Error:** an entry that does not parse or is out of range turns the underline `ink-muted` and strikes the text through at 2px; terms that cannot be computed replace the margin figure with a sentence naming the problem.
- **Behavior:** every valid keystroke updates the whole document; leaving the blank reformats it in the reader's number format; the arrow keys step it (Shift for ten steps); Enter commits.

### Tickbox

- **Style:** a 1.25rem square, 1.5px `ink` border, transparent. Checked, it fills with `mark`, takes a `mark-ink` border and shows a `mark-ink` check (0.875rem, stroke 3); the fill changes over 140ms.

### Ruler

- **Character:** a range input drawn as a ruler, at home on a highlighter field.
- **Style:** full width and 2.75rem tall; a 2px `mark-ink` track; a square 0.625rem by 2.25rem `mark-ink` thumb that widens 1.6 times while held (140ms); an `ew-resize` cursor. Ticks are 1px by 0.5rem with 0.75rem tabular labels under them, placed on the path of the thumb's centre (half a thumb in from each end). The label sits left (1rem, 600), the readout right in the figure voice.
- **Focus:** a 2px `mark-ink` ring at 4px offset.
- **Behavior:** a track that is not linear (the valuation multiple runs on a log scale) steps through the values a person would say out loud when the arrow keys are used.

### Navigation

- **Letterhead:** a 4rem row (4.5rem from 1024px) with the wordmark left at 1.3125rem and, right, the links at 0.9375rem and 500 weight, 1.75rem apart (from 768px), then the primary button; a full-width 1px `ink` rule below. Below 768px only the wordmark and the button remain.
- **Running head:** documents repeat a running head, and this one carries the reader's own figure. A fixed 3.5rem bar on `paper` over a 1px `ink` rule: the wordmark (back to the top) left at 1.0625rem; "Your 10,000 shares: €16,875 vested today" in `ink-soft` with the figure at 700 in `ink`, from 640px; the 2.5rem primary button right. It slides down (260ms) once the opening part has scrolled away and back up (180ms), and is out of the tab order and hidden from assistive technology while away.

### Highlighter Mark

- **Character:** one flat, full-strength field behind the words that matter.
- **Build:** the words are set twice: the base copy in the surrounding ink, and a highlighted copy (`mark` field, `mark-ink` text) laid exactly over it and hidden from assistive technology. Sweeping the copy's clip-path open from the left reveals the highlight, and the words underneath keep their contrast in either theme.
- **Sweeps:** `load` (560ms on `--ease-marker`, after a set delay), `scroll` (linked to the element's view, from 70% of its entry to 42% of its cover), or none.
- **Placement:** one headline word, the reader's key figure, a phrase as it is read, "this year" in the yearly ledger.

### Rolling Figures

- **Character:** the key figures (the margin's figures and share count, the payout, the running head, the redline) own fixed digit slots and roll digit by digit when their value changes; ledger rows and yearly totals simply update.
- **Build:** each digit is a 1em window over a 0 to 9 strip moved with `translateY`; separators and the currency sign stand still; slots are keyed from the right so the units stay put when a figure gains a digit; the full value is given to assistive technology once.
- **Timing:** an edit rolls in 460ms with an 18ms cascade from the units up. A figure introduced on load rolls up from zeros, left to right, over 1200ms with a 70ms stagger (from 900ms in the first viewport). CSS transitions keep it interruptible while the reader types; reduced motion shows the value at once.
- **Fit:** a display figure is sized for a typical length and shrinks in proportion when it runs longer (`--fit` is the designed length over the actual length, at most 1).

### Margin Annotation

- **Style:** right of the vertical rule, a label (0.9375rem, 500), the key figure in the Figure Lead voice under the highlighter, then a short `ink-soft` line (0.9375rem, line-height 1.375, up to 34ch) with the share count at 600 in `ink`. ### Motion

- **Easings:** `--ease-out`, cubic-bezier(0.23, 1, 0.32, 1), for every state change, entrance and roll; `--ease-in-out`, cubic-bezier(0.77, 0, 0.175, 1), for drawing (the letterhead rule, the exhibit's wipe); `--ease-marker`, cubic-bezier(0.65, 0, 0.35, 1), for the highlighter's load sweep only.
- **State changes:** 120ms for a press and a link's underline, 140ms for blanks, the tickbox and the ruler thumb, 160ms for button colors and the arrow.
- **Load sequence, once per surface:** the letterhead rule draws (1000ms, `--ease-in-out`); the wordmark and links appear (600ms, at 100ms and 250ms); from 1024px the headline's lines rise out of their own clip (900ms, at 120ms and 220ms), and below it the whole headline fades up instead (800ms, at 150ms); the lede, clause, margin and signature line fade up from 0.6 opacity and 0.75rem below (800ms, at 350ms to 560ms); the headline word is swept at 850ms and the key figure at 1000ms, as the figure rolls up from zero from 900ms.

- **Gates:** the resting CSS is always the finished state. ## App surfaces

The app (app.vestwise.app, `packages/app`) is an Operate surface of this world: the reader is there to do a task, so navigation and controls stay the standard ones and the world lends its type, palette, density and one signature move. Three things apply there and nowhere else.

**The Gridline Amendment.** In a table the body rows are gridlines, so `hairline` rules them. The head's rule, the 2px rule above a total and every structural rule stay `ink`.

**The State Amendment.** One state colour, `state` (oklch(0.55 0.2 27) by day, oklch(0.7 0.19 22) at night), exists for errors and irreversible actions: the destructive button's rule and label, error text, and the rule around a notice whose action cannot be undone. It never colours a figure; money carries its direction with a sign and a word.

**The Wash.** The app's one derived surface is `wash`, 7% `ink` over `paper` (the exhibit's own wash): the hover of a row or a menu item and the ground of the current page in the sidebar. Nothing else is tinted.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:

- **Do** take every color from the seven theme tokens and let `prefers-color-scheme` swap the set; anything on yellow is `mark-ink`.
- **Do** keep the highlighter a flat, full-strength field tight to its words (0.07em each side, overhanging left only), and move it only with a clip-path wipe.
- **Do** highlight at most one word per headline: the word the sentence turns on, with its full stop.
- **Do** set the agreement in Source Serif 4 and everything VestWise says, computes or offers in Archivo, and put inputs into sentences as blanks.
- **Do** separate with 1px ink rules (2px above a total) and measure the space between blocks in clause lines (2rem; 2.5rem from 1024px).
- **Do** keep every corner square (0px).
- **Do** set numbers in tabular lining figures and the reader's locale, and roll the key figures digit by digit when they change (460ms, 18ms cascade).
- **Do** write the finished state as the resting CSS, and add motion only inside `prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference`, scroll-linked motion only inside `@supports (animation-timeline: view())`, and hover only for `(hover: hover) and (pointer: fine)`.
- **Do** hold a highlighter field to the sheet's 1320px measure at night.
- **Do** theme the browser's own surfaces: selection in `mark` (inverted on a highlighter field), the caret and the 2px focus ring in `ink` (`mark-ink` on a highlighter field), the scrollbar in `ink-muted` on `paper`, and link underlines at a 0.22em offset.

### Don't:

- **Don't** use cards, shadows, blur, glass or gradients; the running head floats on paper above a rule, and nothing else floats.
- **Don't** tint, fade, outline or round the highlighter, draw it as a partial-height marker stroke, or set grey text on it.
- **Don't** add a second accent or a tint of ink or mark; the exhibit's 7% ink wash is the only derived color.
- **Don't** use an em dash or an en dash in visible copy; use a colon, a comma, a full stop or parentheses, and a true minus sign (−) for a subtraction.
- **Don't** set a § number, or any short label, on its own line above a headline; § heads run in at the start of their clause.
- **Don't** set Archivo narrower than 100% or wider than 112%, or track it tighter than -0.03em.
- **Don't** set anything in the figure voice that the page did not compute from its own terms: no stat rows, counts or decorative numbers.
- **Don't** give each part its own entrance; after the one load sequence, motion belongs to reading.
- **Don't** stand Unicode glyphs or emoji in for icons; icons are Lucide line icons at stroke 2 to 3, sized to the text (§, × and − are typography, not icons).

### Extending to a new surface

- **Decide which part of the agreement it is.** New content enters as a part the document already has: a clause with its margin annotation, sub-clauses with glosses, an annex with an exhibit and a yearly ledger, a highlighter field for a calculation, a signature block for the action. Use that part's components as written above; new clauses continue the § numbering.
- **Use the same sheet.** The 1320px frame and gutters, the letterhead and its rule, the clause line, and from 1024px the 12-column grid with the terms left of the rule and VestWise's answer right of it.
- **In the app, stay a product.** Standard navigation and controls in the world's materials (see App surfaces); the world's own interface idioms enter only as the signature move.
- **Spend the yellow sparingly.** At most one headline word, the reader's key figure, the current moment ("Today", "this year"), focus and selection, and a highlighter field only for a page's interactive answer; the landing has one.
- **Keep motion inside the grammar.** A load sequence only where a surface opens, reading devices after it, and nothing outside the gates.
- **Rasters are pages of the document.** The 1200 by 630 share image is set like a first viewport: the day sheet, the letterhead row and rule (wordmark left, domain right), the headline with its one highlighted word, a sample clause with blanks, and the margin figure on the highlighter, labelled as sample.
- **New components inherit the materials.** Square corners, 1px rules, `mark-ink` on `mark`, tabular figures, 140ms to 160ms `--ease-out` state changes and the global focus ring.
